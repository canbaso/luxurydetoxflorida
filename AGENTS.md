# Luxury Detox Florida Editorial Instructions

These rules apply to all editorial, SEO, facility-ranking, medical-information, and WordPress article work in this repository.

## Source of truth

Read `EDITORIAL_WORKFLOW.md` before researching, drafting, revising, or auditing an article.

Do not skip research because a facility, treatment center, law, accreditation, price, or medical fact appeared in an earlier article. Re-verify current information before publication.

## Mandatory research sequence

1. Research the target keyword in Semrush before drafting.
2. Compare the exact keyword with broader and adjacent phrases. Record volume, keyword difficulty, intent, and useful related queries when available.
3. Search the live web for the current SERP and competing article types.
4. For facility-list articles, discover candidate facility names before writing. Build a candidate pool larger than the final list.
5. Verify every selected facility against its current official website and, where relevant, current Florida regulatory or credentialing sources.
6. Verify that the facility actually provides the service named in the article. Do not infer a service from a broad "mental health," "dual diagnosis," "luxury," or "recovery" claim.
7. Use authoritative medical and government sources for clinical, legal, licensing, insurance, and safety claims.

## Facility ranking rule

A "Top 10" article must not begin with a predetermined ten names.

Search first. Build at least 15 credible candidates when the market allows. Then remove facilities that do not meet the article's exact scope.

Do not pad a list to reach ten. If fewer than ten verified facilities qualify, state that rather than including weak or misclassified entries.

Never invent:
- facility names;
- locations;
- programs;
- bed counts;
- pricing;
- private rooms;
- amenities;
- licensing;
- accreditation;
- insurance participation;
- detox capability;
- residential capability;
- FARR status;
- psychiatric staffing;
- medical supervision;
- treatment outcomes.

## Prose rules

Write direct, factual prose. Prefer the precise statement over a clever phrase.

Mannered prose is prohibited. Do not replace a simple statement with decorative metaphor or a phrase designed to display the writer.

Avoid constructions such as:
- "a dial worth turning";
- "this point earns its keep";
- "sits at the intersection of";
- "a beacon of";
- "a journey toward";
- "in today's fast-paced world";
- "when it comes to";
- "whether you are...";
- "this isn't X, it's Y" as a repeated rhetorical device;
- vague emotional filler;
- generic inspirational language.

Do not use em dashes.

Avoid the words: perhaps, imagine, embrace, vital, excellence, perilous, and "chill pill" unless they are unavoidable in a quotation or proper name.

Do not write "many people search for..." or discuss search volume inside consumer-facing article copy.

Do not use telegram-style fragments. Short sentences are encouraged, but paragraphs should still read naturally.

Use people-first language. Do not sensationalize addiction, withdrawal, mental illness, relapse, overdose, or psychiatric symptoms.

## WordPress format

Unless a different format is explicitly requested:
- include one `<h1>`;
- use `<section>` blocks for article structure;
- avoid unnecessary `<div>` elements;
- do not use `<canvas>`, icons, or emojis;
- use inline styles for comparison tables when needed;
- include relevant internal links;
- include a Sources section;
- include FAQPage JSON-LD when FAQs are present;
- include ItemList JSON-LD for ranked facility lists.

See `EDITORIAL_WORKFLOW.md` for the full workflow, research checklist, ranking rubric, prose harness, and article assembly rules.
